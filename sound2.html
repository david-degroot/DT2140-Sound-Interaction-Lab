    • Woahwhoahwhaooh: trumpet room filter

    For Interaction 3 we imagined someone playing the Trumpet in a room. You are getting annoyed at that and want to close the door to not hear it. The phone tilting is linked to a low pass filter on the brass sound effect. When the phone lays flat, the sound is muffled and when you tilt the phone like you are opening a door you can hear the full frequency range.
